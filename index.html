📌 Purpose of this Webpage
This platform monitors global price dynamics, trend momentum, and market valuation across low-Carbon Intensity (CI) feedstocks essential for Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF - HEFA pathway), Renewable Diesel (HVO), and agricultural vegetable oil complexes. All assets are standardized to 1 Metric Ton (1,000 kg) in INR (₹) and USD ($) with real-time 1-Day (1D) and 1-Week (1W) percentage change trends.
💡 Key SAF & Biofuel Competitor Benchmarks
  • Used Cooking Oil (UCO / Yellow Grease): The primary non-edible waste feedstock for HEFA SAF and Renewable Diesel worldwide.
  • Technical Tallow (Animal Fats Cat 1/2/3): A major non-edible waste lipid competing directly with plant-based waste oils.
  • Distillers Corn Oil (DCO / Inedible Corn Oil): A low-CI inedible byproduct from ethanol processing.
  • Palm Oil Mill Effluent (POME / PAO): Non-edible waste residue from tropical oil milling.
  • CBOT Soybean & Canola Oil: Primary agricultural liquid oil baselines that set the price ceiling for virgin vegetable oil hydrotreating.
  • Gasoil / Heating Oil & Brent Crude: Energy market anchors that dictate economic parity and margin spreads between petroleum and bio-feedstocks.
