📌 Purpose of this Webpage
This platform was established as a central market tracking hub to monitor price movements, trend directions, and valuation relationships across global vegetable oil futures, energy markets, and Indian domestic spot markets. Because international commodities trade in varying native units (¢/lb, $/barrel, $/gallon), this dashboard normalizes every price into a standardized 1 Metric Ton (1,000 kg) reference in both INR (₹) and USD ($) alongside real-time 1-Day (1D) and 1-Week (1W) percentage change trends.
💡 Key Market Benchmarks Included
  • CBOT Soybean Oil (Chicago): The primary global anchor for liquid vegetable oils. It defines international price direction for food-grade liquid oils and biofuel feedstocks.
  • Crude Palm Oil / FCPO (Bursa Malaysia): The world's largest volume vegetable oil. Its futures contract sets the baseline floor price for global vegetable fats and tropical oils.
  • Gasoil / Heating Oil (NYMEX): Reflects refined petroleum diesel values, tracking economic demand shifts between the energy sector and agricultural markets.
  • Brent Crude Oil (ICE): The global macro benchmark for crude oil, influencing production, refining, and freight costs across all commodities.
  • Canola / Rapeseed Oil (MATIF / ICE): The primary high-oleic liquid oil reference in Europe and North America.
  • Indian Domestic Spot Benchmarks (SEA & NCDEX): Captures real-time domestic supply-demand dynamics, import duty impacts, and local harvest yields across India (Refined Soy, Mustard, Groundnut, Rice Bran, and Castor).
